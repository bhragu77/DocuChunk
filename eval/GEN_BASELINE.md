## How to read this

- **context-recall / context-precision are deterministic** — computed from the retrieval ground truth, not a model. They are meaningful in every profile and are the metrics that catch a *retrieval* regression.
- **faithfulness** answers "is the answer true to the sources?" — the same question the live groundedness check answers, but batched and scored.
- **answer-relevancy** answers "did it actually address the question?" — the axis the live check cannot see: a true-but-off-topic answer scores high on faithfulness yet low here.
- **The `surrogate` profile is deterministic and hermetic** (no API, no torch) so CI can gate on it; its faithfulness/relevancy are lexical stand-ins. Re-run with `--provider gemini` for the neural numbers.

> Real generation (production generate_answer) scored by an LLM judge; faithfulness reuses the live groundedness check.
